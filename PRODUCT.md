## Product Principles

1. **Outcome First, Mechanism Second:** State the business relief ("Never let a closed deal go cold") before diving into the node architecture.
2. **Prove with Real Artifacts:** Showcase actual screen captures, data flows, and verified schemas instead of abstract illustrations or placeholder art.
3. **Human-in-the-Loop as a Feature:** Emphasize that every automated system requires human review before money moves, emails send, or data is committed.
4. **Radical Honesty as a Moat:** Never fudge numbers, never simulate client adoption, and transparently label models, datasets, and architecture origins.
5. **Zero Slop:** No gratuitous neon gradients, no generic card-in-card containers, no empty jargon. Every visual element has a functional purpose.
