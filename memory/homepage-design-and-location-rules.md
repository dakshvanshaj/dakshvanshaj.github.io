# Homepage design & location rules for dakshvanshaj.com

Confirmed directly with the site owner while shaping the homepage portfolio:

## Location & Identity
- **Location:** Just say **"India"**. Do not add specific towns, states, or "open to relocation" on the homepage. Keep it simple and clean.
- **Photo / Profiles:** Do not show portrait photo on the homepage; replace with direct connect links to LinkedIn (https://www.linkedin.com/in/daksh-vanshaj/) and GitHub (https://github.com/dakshvanshaj), confirmed directly with site owner.
- **Resume:** Point to `assets/resume/Daksh_Vanshaj_AI_Automation_Engineer.pdf` (sourced from `projects/jobs/resumes/Daksh_Vanshaj_AI_Automation_Engineer.pdf`).
- **Narada OS Screenshots:** Use the updated interface captures from the Straight Answer Agent repository (`issues.png` for nightly diagnostics and `reorder.png` for propose-only orders).

## Aesthetic & Anti-Slop Direction
- **Refuse the sci-fi AI tropes:** No HUDs, glowing neon borders, node-graph canvases with pulsing particles, or terminal consoles pretending to be "system active". The owner explicitly rejected this as AI slop.
- **Clean Architectural Studio Ground:** Refined neutral canvas (`#F8FAFC` ground, `#F1F5F9` surfaces, `#FFFFFF` cards) — crisp, high contrast, and avoids the generic warm-beige/parchment AI trope.
- **Typography:** Warm literary editorial serif for headlines (`Literata`) paired with clean human grotesque (`Figtree`). Avoid overused defaults (Inter, Plus Jakarta Sans, Geist, Roboto, Fraunces).
- **Structure:** Linear cadence with generous vertical rhythm. Remove chapter/section numbers (`01`, `02`, `03`) unless sequence carries vital information.
- **Narada OS presentation:** Give it real visual weight (tabbed real screenshots + 2-minute demo video) as a capability showcase, not a sales pitch.
